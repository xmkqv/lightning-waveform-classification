# Data policy

This repository does not include or license a waveform dataset. The test suite
creates synthetic arrays and does not depend on external observations.

Experiments expect ten NumPy arrays under `datasets/waveform/lightning`. Before
using a dataset, record its owner, source, license or agreement, permitted uses,
and any publication restrictions outside the Git repository. Do not commit the
arrays, credentials, provider documents, or generated reports.

rights to third-party datasets or to results derived from them.
